Auto-schedule only plans for branches that have opening hours. Staffing needs say how many people you need and when.

## Steps
1. Open **Schedules**, then the **Setup** tab.
2. Find **Opening hours and staffing needs**.
3. Tap **Branch setup**.
   ![Setup tab](shot:schedules-setup-tab)
4. Choose the branch at the top.
5. Under **Opening hours**, set the open and close time for each day. Untick a day to mark it **Closed**.
6. Tap "Use ... for all open days" to copy times.
7. Under **Staffing needs**, tap **+ Add staffing need**.
8. Set **People needed**, the role, the day, and the **from** and **to** times.
   ![Branch setup panel](shot:branch-setup-sheet)
9. Tap **Save** with the branch name.

## What happens next
The app says "Saved opening hours and staffing needs for ...". Saving replaces all needs for that branch.

With no staffing needs, the scheduler plans 1 person for the whole opening time. A branch with no opening hours shows "Not set up yet — nothing will be auto-scheduled here". Times that end after midnight show **next day**.

In **Setup**, admins also find **Staff self-service**, where a tick box lets staff hand shifts to each other. A manager still approves each handover. Owners also see a tick box for cross-branch claims.

## Common problems
- **"No opening hours yet" in Auto-schedule.** Set them here first.
- **Unsaved changes.** The panel shows "Unsaved changes". Tap **Save** before closing.
- **Staff cannot ask for cover.** Tick the shift handover box in **Staff self-service**.
