Welcome to Third State HR. You use it to see your shifts, clock in and out, ask for leave, upload documents and read your payslips. This guide gets you started.

## Steps
1. Open the invitation email from your manager.
2. Tap the link in the email.
3. Type a **New password** (at least 8 characters).
   ![Set your password page](shot:set-password-form)
4. Type the same password again in **Confirm password**.
5. Tap **Save password**.
6. Wait for "Password saved. Taking you to your workspace…".
7. Look at your **Home** screen. It says "Hi" and your first name.
8. If you see **Your manager needs something**, tap the big button under it.
   ![Staff Home screen](shot:staff-home)
9. Check **Next shifts** to see your coming shifts.
10. Check **Leave balance** to see your days left.
11. On a phone, use the bar at the bottom: **Home**, **Schedule**, **Clock**, **Leave**, and **More**.
12. On a computer, use the menu on the left.

## What happens next
From now on you sign in with your work email and your new password.

Staff see these menu items: Home, Schedule, Clock, Leave, Documents, Payslips, Profile and Notifications. If you are new, you also see **Onboarding** until your joining checklist is closed. Shift supervisors also see **Today** and **Attendance**. Some items only show when they apply to you, such as **My Interviews**.

The bell at the top shows your notifications. Tap your name at the top to open your profile.

## Common problems
- **The link says it is invalid or has expired.** Each link works once. Ask your manager to send the invite again, or use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.
- **I see "No workspace assigned yet".** Your login is not linked to a branch. Ask your owner or admin to finish your access setup.
- **I see "Your access is inactive".** Your account was switched off. Contact your manager or Company Admin.
- **Can I install it on my phone?** We could not confirm this in the app. You can open the website in your phone browser and sign in there.
