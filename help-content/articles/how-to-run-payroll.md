The **Payroll** page runs the whole month in one place. The order is: month, employees, salary preparation, check and approve, pay date, payments, payslips. Every amount opens to show how it was calculated.

## Steps
1. Tap **Payroll** in the menu.
2. Choose the month in **Payroll month**. For a new month, pick it in **Start another month** and tap **Prepare payroll**.
   ![Payroll month and prepare](shot:payroll-month-prepare)
3. Read the checklist at the top. Fix anything marked **Must fix**. Items marked **Worth a look** are only warnings.
4. Look at **What changed since** last month. Tap **Explain in plain words** if you want.
5. Check the table: **Fixed pay**, **Extra earnings**, **Deductions**, **Net pay** and **Status**. Rows marked **Fix needed** or **Check** need attention.
6. Tap a person to open the record. Check **Hours this month**, **Earnings**, **Deductions** and **Pay and allowances**.
   ![Payroll record for one employee](shot:payroll-record-drawer)
7. If hours or pay setup changed, tap **Recalculate**.
8. In **Pay date and payslips**, tap **Change dates** to set the pay date and when payslips are published.
9. Tick **Select all** in the table, or tick some people.
10. Tap **Approve**. In review mode tap **Send for review** first.
    ![Payroll bulk actions](shot:payroll-bulk-bar)
11. Then record payments and publish payslips.

## What happens next
Each person shows one status: **Not prepared**, **Draft**, **In review**, **Approved**, then **Published** once the payslip is shared. A person can show **Fix needed** or **Check**.

The top boxes show **Employees**, **Total net payroll**, **Paid** (and how much is "still to pay") and **Needs a look**. When everyone is paid and published the page says "This month is finished".

How approval works depends on **Settings**: **Two people: one prepares, another approves**, **Prepare → review → approve**, or Owner single step. In the standard setting a second person must approve.

Prepare uses salary, allowances, leave, overtime, tips and advances. Branch managers enter hours and tips only. They never see pay amounts.

Other buttons at the top: **Distribute tips**, **Advances**, **Reports** and **Settings**.

## Common problems
- **"Fix needed" on someone.** Open them and read the red items. Common ones are missing pay or bank details.
- **Approve is missing or greyed.** You may not have approval permission, or the person is not sent for review. The record says why.
- **I am the one who prepared it.** In the standard setting a second person must approve.
- **A message says payroll uses the standard settings.** Tap **Review settings** and check them once before your first payroll.
- **Someone is "Not prepared".** Tap **Prepare** on their row.
