Leave requests wait for you on the **Leave** page. A Branch Manager decides for their branch. Company Admins and Owners can decide for the whole company.

## Steps
1. Tap **Leave** in the menu.
2. Look at **Awaiting your decision**.
   ![Leave awaiting your decision](shot:leave-awaiting-decision)
3. Read the name, dates and days asked.
4. Tap **Approve** or **Reject**.
5. For **Reject**, confirm "Reject ...’s leave?".
6. If the person has too few days, you see "Not enough leave balance." Admins can tap **Override & approve…**, type the **Override reason (required)** and tap **Confirm override**.
7. Check **Recent decisions** at the bottom.

## What happens next
Approving says "Leave approved — balance updated." Rejecting says "Leave rejected." The employee gets a notification.

If the person still has shifts on those days, a yellow box says they were not cancelled. Tap **Find cover** for each one. Shifts not shared yet will not be shared with staff on leave days.

Status words are **Pending**, **Approved**, **Rejected** and **Cancelled**.

## Common problems
- **Approve fails with "Insufficient balance".** Reject the request, or ask a Company Admin or Owner to override.
- **I do not see any requests.** Branch Managers only see their own branch.
- **The person says it is still pending.** Check it is not in another branch's queue.
