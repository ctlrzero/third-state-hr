**Today** shows your branch right now. It lists who is working, who is late, open shifts and approvals. It updates by itself.

## Steps
1. Tap **Today** in the menu.
2. Owners and Company Admins choose a branch in the **Branch** box. Others see their own branch.
3. Read the four counts at the top: **Scheduled**, **In now**, **Not in** and **Approvals**.
   ![Today board](shot:today-board)
4. Look at **Needs you now**. For each person tap **Find cover**, or use the phone link to call them.
5. For a missing clock-out, tap **Fix**. For a clock-in with no shift, tap **Review**.
6. Open **Waiting for your approval** to approve or reject swaps, or tap **Review** for leave and documents.
7. Tap **Refresh** to update the board.

## What happens next
The board groups people as: **Needs you now**, **Working now**, **Later today**, **Finished** and **On leave**.

Badges you may see: **Can’t come in**, **No show**, **Not in** (with minutes late), **Missing clock-out**, **Clock-in without a shift**, **Unfilled**, **Not shared · unfilled** and **Offer sent · waiting**.

If shifts today are not shared with staff, a yellow note says so. Managers can tap **Share now**. Supervisors are told to ask their manager.

Shift supervisors see **Today** and **Attendance** for their own branch. They cannot build schedules.

## Common problems
- **No branch to show.** You are not assigned to a branch yet. Ask your admin.
- **Someone is on the board as Not in but is working.** They may have forgotten to clock in. Correct it in **Attendance**.
- **I cannot open the shift to edit it.** Supervisors can find cover only. Managers can edit on **Schedules**.
