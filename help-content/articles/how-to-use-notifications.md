Notifications tell you when something needs you or has been decided, for example a leave decision. The bell at the top shows how many are unread.

## Steps
1. Tap the bell at the top of the screen.
   ![Notification bell with unread count](shot:notifications-bell)
2. Tap a notification to open it. The app takes you to the related page, such as **Leave** or **Schedule**.
3. Tap **Mark all read** to clear the list.
4. For the full list, open **Notifications** in the menu.
   ![Notifications page](shot:notifications-page)
5. Tick **Show unread only** to hide the ones you have read.
6. Tap **Mark all as read** to clear them all.
7. Tap **Load older** to see older ones.

## What happens next
A notification is marked read when you open it. Important ones show **High priority**. When the list is empty you see "You're all caught up".

## Common problems
- **I opened a notification but nothing happened.** Some notifications have no page to open. The message itself is the information.
- **I cannot find an old notification.** Tap **Load older** at the bottom.
