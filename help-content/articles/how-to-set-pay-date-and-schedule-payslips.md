Each month has a pay date and a payslip publishing plan. Staff only see a payslip after it is published.

## Steps
1. Open **Payroll** and choose the month.
2. Find **Pay date and payslips**. It shows **Pay date** and **Payslips**.
   ![Pay date and payslips panel](shot:payroll-pay-date-panel)
3. Tap **Change dates**.
4. Set **Pay date (when the salary goes to the bank)**. It can be in the future. Leave it empty to use the payroll settings.
5. Set **Publish payslips on (Dubai time)**. Leave it empty to publish by hand.
6. Tick **Only publish payslips for people whose payment is recorded** if you want.
7. Tap **Save dates**.
8. To publish by hand: tick the approved people, tap **Publish payslips** and confirm.
9. To stop a schedule, tap **Stop automatic publishing**.
   ![Change dates form](shot:payroll-change-dates)

## What happens next
The page says "Payslips will be published on ..." for everyone approved (and paid, if you ticked that box). Employees are notified and can view and download their payslip under **Payslips**.

If no pay date is set, the page shows "(from payroll settings)". The usual pay day is set in **Settings** as "Pay day (of the following month)".

A published payslip cannot be edited. Use a correction. Payslip lists also appear in **Download payslips** in the bulk bar.

## Common problems
- **Payslips did not publish on time.** Check people were Approved, and Paid if you ticked the box.
- **Staff say they have no payslip.** It is not published yet.
- **Settings has a "Note printed on payslips" box.** Use it for a message on every payslip.
