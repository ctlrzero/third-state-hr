Repeating shifts save time for fixed rotas. Auto-schedule (admins only) builds a draft roster for you. Both create shifts that are not shared until you share them.

## Steps
To use repeating shifts (Branch Managers and admins):
1. Open **Schedules**, then the **Repeating shifts** tab. Admins see it as **Setup**.
2. Tap **New repeating shift**.
3. Choose **Branch**, **Employee**, **Day of week**, **Start**, **End** and **Break (min)**.
4. Set **Effective from**. Add **Effective to (optional)** if it ends.
   ![New repeating shift form](shot:schedules-repeating-form)
5. Save it.
6. In **Weekly repeating shifts**, choose a **Branch**, **Period start** and **Period end**.
7. Tap **Create shifts**.
8. Check them on the **Week** tab.
9. Tap **Share with staff** and confirm.

To use Auto-schedule (Company Admin and Owner):
1. Open **Schedules**, then the **Auto-schedule** tab.
2. Step 1: choose **Next week**, **Next 7 days**, **Next 2 weeks** or **Custom**.
3. Step 2: tick the branches. Tap **Set up** if a branch has no opening hours.
4. Step 3: tap **Preview plan**.
   ![Auto-schedule preview](shot:auto-schedule-preview)
5. Read the plan.
6. Tap **Create** to save the shifts as "(not shared yet)".
7. Go to the **Week** tab and share the week.

## What happens next
Auto-schedule plans from opening hours, staffing needs, availability, approved leave and weekly limits (48 hours, 6 days). People from the home branch go first. Nothing is saved until you create the shifts. If you change the period or branches, tap **Update preview** first.

Shifts that would overlap existing shifts are skipped. The app says how many were created.

## Common problems
- **"No opening hours yet".** Set opening hours first. See the opening hours guide.
- **"No new shifts".** Everyone already has these shifts, or they would overlap.
- **I do not see Auto-schedule.** It is only for Company Admins and Owners.
