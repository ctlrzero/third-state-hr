Use your work email and password to sign in. If you forgot your password, the app emails you a link to choose a new one.

## Steps
1. Open the app. You see **Sign in to your workspace**.
2. Type your **Work email**.
3. Type your **Password**. Tap **Show** to check what you typed.
   ![Sign in page](shot:sign-in-form)
4. Tap **Sign in**.

To reset a forgotten password:

1. On the sign-in page, tap **Forgot password?**
   ![Forgot password link](shot:sign-in-forgot-link)
2. Check your **Work email** is right.
3. Tap **Send reset link**.
4. Open the email and tap the link. Check your spam folder if it is not there.
5. Type a **New password** and **Confirm password**.
6. Tap **Save password**.

## What happens next
The app saves your new password and takes you to your workspace. The reset link can only be used once.

Your password needs at least 8 characters and cannot be only spaces. You cannot reuse a password from this account.

If you are signed in for a long time, the app may say "Your session has expired. Please sign in again." Just sign in again.

## Common problems
- **"Invalid email or password. Please try again."** Check your email and password. Use **Forgot password?** if you are not sure.
- **"Your account has not been activated yet."** Open the invitation email and use its link first.
- **"Too many attempts. Please wait a minute and try again."** Wait one minute and try again.
- **"We couldn't reach the server."** Check your internet connection.
- **I never got the reset email.** The app shows the same message for every email address. Check spam, then ask your manager or admin to resend your invite.
- **I cannot sign up myself.** Self sign-up is not available. Your owner or admin creates your login.
