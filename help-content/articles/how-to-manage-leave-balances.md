Leave days go down when leave is approved. You can fix a balance by hand, or set up accrual so days are added each month or year.

## Steps
To adjust one person's balance:
1. Open **People** and tap the person.
2. Open the **Leave** tab.
3. Under **Balances**, tap **Adjust** under the leave type.
   ![Employee leave balances](shot:leave-balances-adjust)
4. Type the **New balance (days)**.
5. Type the reason.
6. Tap **Save balance**.

To set up accrual:
1. Open **Leave**. Scroll to **Leave accrual policies**.
2. Tap **Configure policy**.
3. Choose **Leave type**, **Frequency** (Monthly or Annual) and **Days per period**.
4. Choose **Rounding**: No rounding, Nearest half day or Nearest day.
5. Save the policy.
6. An Owner taps **Approve** on the policy.
7. Type **Period to run** (for example 2026-08 for monthly, or 2026 for annual).
8. Tap **Run accrual**.
   ![Leave accrual policies](shot:leave-accrual-policies)

## What happens next
A new policy shows **Pending** until the Owner approves it ("Awaiting Owner approval"). It is never automatic. You must tap **Run accrual** each period. The app says how many employees were credited.

Adjustments need a reason and are kept in the history.

## Common problems
- **"Enter a valid number of days (0 or more)."** Type a number such as 21.
- **I cannot run accrual.** The policy is not approved yet. Ask the Owner.
- **A banner says "pending HR/legal approval".** The seeded leave values are not final. Check them with HR or legal.
