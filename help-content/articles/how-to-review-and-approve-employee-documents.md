When an employee uploads a document, someone else must review it. Use the **Document register** to see everything and decide.

## Steps
1. Tap **Documents** in the menu.
2. Stay on the **Document register** tab.
3. Use **Review status** and set it to **Pending review**. You can also search or filter by **Document type** and expiry.
   ![Document register](shot:docs-register)
4. Tap **Review** on a row.
5. Tap **View file** to open it.
6. Tap **Approve** if it is correct.
7. Or tap **Reject**, type the **Rejection reason (required)** and confirm.
   ![Review a document](shot:docs-review-drawer)

Other actions on an approved document:
- **View** opens the file.
- **Renew** starts a renewal on their behalf.
- **Archive** keeps it on record but stops it showing as current.
- **Discard** removes a pending upload that was never made current. This cannot be undone.

## What happens next
The document shows **Approved** or **Rejected**. The employee sees the rejection reason and can resubmit. An approved document stays **Current**. Expiry is shown separately as **Expiring soon**, **Expired** or **No expiry set**.

The second-person rule: you cannot review your own upload. The app says "You uploaded this document, so someone else must review it." The only exception is the Owner. Owner uploads are approved at once.

Branch Managers can only review ordinary documents such as a health card. Passport, visa, Emirates ID, contracts, offer letters, bank and salary documents need a Company Admin or Owner, and are hidden from Branch Managers. A Company Admin's own upload of a sensitive document needs the Owner.

An Owner can switch on "Let Company Admins approve their own uploads". It only covers ordinary documents.

## Common problems
- **I cannot approve it.** You uploaded it, or it is a sensitive document and you are a Branch Manager.
- **Some document types are missing for me.** Branch Managers cannot see sensitive types.
- **"This employee already has a current document of that type."** Use **Renew** on the existing one.
- **Only PDF, JPG and PNG are accepted.** Ask for a different file type.
