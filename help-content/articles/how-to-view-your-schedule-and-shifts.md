Open **Schedule** to see the shifts your manager has planned for you. You only see shifts that your manager has shared with staff.

## Steps
1. Tap **Schedule** in the menu. The page is called **My schedule**.
2. Look at **Upcoming shifts**. Each row shows date, time, branch and job.
   ![My schedule page with upcoming shifts](shot:my-schedule-upcoming)
3. To see more shifts, look at **Open shifts**. These are shifts nobody has taken yet.
4. Tap **Take this shift** on an open shift you want.
5. To start work, tap **Open Clock**.
6. On Home, **Next shifts** shows your next five shifts. Tap **Full schedule** to come here.

If you cannot work a shift:

1. On the shift row, tap **Can’t come in**.
2. Choose a **Reason**.
3. Add a note if you want.
4. Tap **Tell my manager**.
   ![Can't come in form](shot:my-schedule-cant-come-in)

## What happens next
Your manager sees new shifts as "Not shared yet" while they are still a draft. You cannot see them. Once the manager shares them ("Shared with staff"), they appear on your schedule.

If a shift changes or is cancelled, your manager must give a reason, and the change is kept in the history.

When you tap **Can’t come in**, your manager is told straight away and finds cover. A leave request is sent for that day for approval. The shift row then shows **Leave requested**, and **On leave** once it is approved.

A shift on an approved leave day is not shared with you.

## Common problems
- **I cannot see my shifts.** Your manager may not have shared the week yet. Ask them.
- **Home says "No upcoming shifts published yet."** The same: nothing is shared for you yet.
- **I do not see Can’t come in.** It is hidden for shifts that already ended today, and for shifts on a leave day.
- **"Leave types aren’t set up yet".** Call your manager.
- **I want a colleague to take my shift.** See the cover guide below.
