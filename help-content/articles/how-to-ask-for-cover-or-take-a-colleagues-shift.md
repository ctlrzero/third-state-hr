If you cannot work a shift, you can ask a colleague to cover it. You can also pick up shifts that others need covered. A manager must approve every swap.

## Steps
To ask for cover:
1. Open **Schedule**.
2. Find the shift in **Upcoming shifts**.
3. Tap **Ask for cover**.
4. Type a **Note for whoever picks this up (optional)**.
5. Tap **Send request**.
   ![Ask for cover note box](shot:cover-ask)

To take a colleague's shift:
1. Scroll to **Colleagues need cover**.
2. Find a shift you can work.
3. Tap **I’ll cover it**.
   ![Colleagues need cover list](shot:cover-colleagues)

To pick up an unfilled shift:
1. Scroll to **Open shifts**.
2. Tap **Take this shift**.

To answer a manager's offer:
1. At the top, look for **Can you cover?**
2. Tap **Accept shift** to take it, or **Decline**.
   ![Can you cover offers](shot:cover-offers)

To cancel your cover request:
1. Scroll to **Your cover requests**.
2. Tap **Cancel** next to the request.

## What happens next
Your request shows one of these words:
- **Waiting for a colleague**: nobody has taken it yet.
- **Waiting for manager**: a colleague said they will cover it. A manager must now approve.
- **Approved**: the shift is moved to your colleague.
- **Not approved**: the shift stays yours.
- **Cancelled**: you cancelled it.

You can cancel while it is "Waiting for a colleague" or "Waiting for manager". If you cancel, the shift stays yours.

When you accept a manager's offer, the app says "Thanks — the shift is yours." and the shift moves to your upcoming shifts. If you decline, your manager is told.

## Common problems
- **I do not see Ask for cover.** Your company may have shift handovers switched off. You will not see **Colleagues need cover** either. Ask your manager.
- **The app will not let me take a shift.** The app tells you the reason, for example you already work then or are on leave.
- **"This offer is closed."** Someone else took it or the manager withdrew it.
- **I am sick today.** Use **Can’t come in** instead. See the schedule guide.
