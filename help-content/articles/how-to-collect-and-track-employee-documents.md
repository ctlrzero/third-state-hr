The **Employee checklist** shows which documents each person has, and which are missing, rejected or expiring.

## Steps
1. Open **Documents**.
2. Tap the **Employee checklist** tab.
3. Choose an employee.
   ![Employee checklist](shot:docs-checklist)
4. Read each status: **Missing**, **Pending review**, **Approved**, **Rejected**, **Expiring**, **Archived** or **Waived**.
5. To upload for them, tap **Upload on behalf**.
6. Choose the file and tap **Upload**.
7. If the document is not needed, tap **Waive**, type a **Reason** and confirm.
8. To ask the person to upload it, use **Ask for missing details** on their record.

## What happens next
An upload on someone's behalf says "Uploaded and submitted for line manager review." Another person must still approve it.

A waived item shows **Waived** with your reason.

Admins also see **Clean up incomplete uploads**. It removes uploads started but never finished, older than 24 hours. Saved documents are not touched.

## Common problems
- **"No document requirements set up".** Requirements are created at onboarding. None were found for this person.
- **I want the employee to upload it themselves.** Use **Ask for missing details**.
