Onboarding takes a new hire from offer to their first weeks. You start it, invite the person, review what they send, and activate them.

## Steps
1. Tap **Onboarding** in the menu.
2. Tap **Start onboarding**. For an accepted offer start from **Recruiting** instead.
   ![Onboarding dashboard](shot:onboarding-dashboard)
3. Choose **Direct hire** or **Existing pre-boarding employee**.
4. For a direct hire fill in **Full name (as on passport)**, **Email**, **Mobile**, **Employment type**, **Branch**, **Job**, **Start date** and **Reporting manager**.
   ![Start onboarding form](shot:onboarding-start-form)
5. Tap **Start onboarding**.
6. Open the person from **New starters**. Tap **Invite to portal**.
7. Under **Starting pay** tap **Enter pay**. Someone else with payroll approval must tap **Approve pay**.
8. Review **Waiting for your review**. Tap **Approve** or **Request changes** for each part.
9. Review **Documents**: **Approve**, **Reject**, **Mark done** or **Upload contract**.
10. Check **Salary payment details**. Tap **Verify** or **Reject**.
11. Fix anything under **Problems**. Tap **Resolve** when done.
12. Tap **Approve and activate** when it is ready.
    ![Onboarding workspace](shot:onboarding-workspace)

## What happens next
The new person gets an email invitation, and signs in to complete their joining steps. The list tabs show where everyone is: **All open**, **Starting soon**, **Waiting for employee**, **To review**, **Blocked**, **Ready to activate**, **Started**, **Working, onboarding not finished**, **Completed** and **Cancelled**.

Statuses include Setting up, Waiting for employee, Under review, Changes required, Blocked, Ready to activate, Activated, Day one, First weeks and Completed.

**Approve and activate** is only on when nothing blocks it. If you must activate anyway, use **Activate anyway**. Then **Record day one** and finally **Close onboarding**.

Other buttons: **Resend invitation** or **Reissue invitation**, **Raise a problem** (tick "Blocks activation until resolved" if needed), **Cancel onboarding**. A person who never starts can be recorded with **Record withdrawal**.

Other tabs: **Probation**, **Visas & permits** (admins), **Reports** and **Setup** (admins) for the checklist templates.

## Common problems
- **Approve and activate is greyed.** The hover text says "Resolve the blocking items first".
- **The invitation expired.** Tap **Reissue invitation**.
- **I cannot approve the pay I entered.** A different person with payroll approval must do it.
- **No email address.** Add one in **Setup** first. The page says so.
