Tips, advances and one-off items change the amount a person is paid. Add them before you approve the month.

## Steps
To share tips:
1. Open **Payroll** and tap **Distribute tips**.
2. Choose the **Branch** and the **From** and **To** dates.
3. Choose how tips are split: **Equal shares** or **By role points**.
4. Choose how tips reach staff.
5. Check the preview and tap **Confirm distribution**.
   ![Distribute tips panel](shot:payroll-tips-drawer)

To record a salary advance:
1. Tap **Advances**.
2. Under **New advance**, choose the **Employee**, **Amount**, **Paid out on** and **Repayments start**.
3. Set the **Number of instalments** and a **Reason**.
4. Tap **Save advance**.
   ![Salary advances panel](shot:payroll-advances-drawer)

To add a one-off earning or deduction:
1. Select people in the table.
2. Tap **Adjust**. The panel is called **Add earning or deduction**.
3. Choose **Type**, **What for** and **Amount**.
4. Choose **This amount for each employee** or **Split this total across them**.
5. Type the **Reason (shown on the payslip)**.
6. Tap **Preview**, then tap **Add to** and the number of employees.

## What happens next
Advance repayments are taken from payroll automatically. The advance form reminds you that written consent is needed and no interest may be charged.

One-off items appear in the person's record under **One-off earnings and deductions**, and on the payslip. Earnings include Bonus, Commission, Reimbursement, **Correction (underpaid)**, End-of-service gratuity, Leave encashment, Notice pay and Other earning. Deductions include Cash shortage, Uniform, Damage, Approved fine and Other deduction. Damage and fines have legal limits shown in the list.

Role points for tips are set in **Settings**.

## Common problems
- **Some positions have no role points.** One point is used. Set points in **Settings**.
- **Preview shows 0.** Nobody selected can take the item. Check the status of each person.
- **I added a wrong item.** Open the person and tap **Remove** next to it, if they are still in Draft.
