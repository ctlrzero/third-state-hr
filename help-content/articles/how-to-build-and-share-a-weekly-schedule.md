The **Schedules** page shows a week board. You add shifts, check them, then share them with staff. Staff cannot see a shift until you share it.

## Steps
1. Tap **Schedules** in the menu.
2. Choose your branch in **Branch**.
3. Use the arrows and **This week** to pick the week.
   ![Schedules week board](shot:schedules-week-board)
4. Tap **New shift**.
5. Choose the **Branch**, **Date**, **Start** and **End**.
6. Set **Break (min)** and **Role (optional)** if needed.
7. In **Assign to (optional — leave blank to post as open)**, choose a person. Leave it blank for an open shift.
   ![New shift form](shot:schedules-new-shift)
8. Tap **Create shift**.
9. Check the week board. Shifts that are not shared show **Not shared**.
10. Tap **Share week with staff** and confirm. You must choose one branch first.
    ![Share week with staff button](shot:schedules-share-week)

## What happens next
New shifts are drafts. On the board they show **Not shared**. The top of the page says how many are "not shared yet".

After you share, staff see them. The board then shows **Shared**. A shift you changed after sharing shows **Changed**. A cancelled shift shows **Cancelled**.

The app says "Shared ... shift(s) with staff." If someone is on approved leave on that day, their shift is not shared. The app lists who, so you can reassign or cancel it.

If a work pattern is broken the form shows a warning: "You can still save — this is a heads-up, not a block."

## Common problems
- **I cannot see Share week with staff.** Choose a single branch in **Branch**. It does not show when "All branches" is chosen.
- **The shift is on someone who is on leave.** The board says "shift(s) held by someone on approved leave — reassign or cancel."
- **Staff say they cannot see their shifts.** They are probably still **Not shared**. Share the week.
- **I want to repeat shifts every week.** See the repeating shifts guide.
