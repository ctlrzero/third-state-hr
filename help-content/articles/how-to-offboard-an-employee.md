Offboarding keeps a person's leaving in one place: notice, last working day, the leaving checklist and the final settlement. Branch Managers can see the cases for their branch. Company Admins and Owners start them.

## Steps
1. Tap **Offboarding** in the menu. You can also open a person's record and tap **Start offboarding**.
2. Tap **Start offboarding**.
   ![Offboarding list](shot:offboarding-list)
3. Choose the **Employee**.
4. Choose the **Type**, for example Resignation, Termination with notice or End of limited contract.
5. Check **Ended by**.
6. Set **Notice given on** and **Last working day**.
7. Tick **Leaving the UAE (not joining another UAE employer)** if it applies.
8. If the notice is short, answer **Why is the notice shorter?**
9. Type the **Reason**. Only HR sees it, not the branch manager.
10. Tap **Start offboarding** to confirm.
    ![Start offboarding form](shot:offboarding-start-form)
11. Open the case. Work through the **Checklist**. Tap **Done** on each item, or mark one **Not needed** with a reason.
12. Under **Final settlement**, tap **Start settlement in Payroll**. Then finish the payment in **Payroll**.
13. When everything is done, tap **Finish offboarding**.
    ![Offboarding case](shot:offboarding-case)

## What happens next
The list has three tabs: **Leaving**, **Left** and **Cancelled**. Starting offboarding sets the last working day for payroll and creates the leaving checklist.

The final settlement should be paid within 14 days of the last working day. The page shows the due date. An off-cycle payroll run opens. When it is paid the checklist item completes by itself.

**Finish offboarding** only works after the last working day and when the required checklist is finished. Use **Change dates** if the dates move. Use **Cancel offboarding** if the person stays.

## Common problems
- **Finish offboarding is greyed.** Hover to read: "After the last working day" or "Finish the required checklist first".
- **I do not see Start offboarding.** Only Company Admins and Owners can start it.
- **Payroll cannot be opened.** You need payroll access, or ask a payroll admin.
