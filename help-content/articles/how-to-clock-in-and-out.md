The Clock screen has one big button. It records when you start and finish work. Your clock times are used to check your attendance.

## Steps
1. Open **Clock**. Managers and admins see it as **My clock**.
2. Look at **Today's shift** to check your planned time.
3. Tap **Clock in** when you start your shift.
   ![Clock screen before clocking in](shot:clock-before)
4. Wait for "Clocked in at ... Saved."
5. When you finish, tap **Clock out**.
   ![Clock screen while clocked in](shot:clock-in-progress)
6. Wait for "Clocked out at ... Saved."
7. Scroll down to **My last 30 days** to check your times.
   ![My last 30 days list](shot:clock-history)

## What happens next
The screen shows **Clocked in** and **Clocked out** times. After you clock out the button says **Shift complete**.

The small label on Home and Clock shows your state: Not clocked in, Clocked in, Clocked out, or No shift today.

In **My last 30 days** each day shows Planned and Actual times, hours worked and how many minutes late you were. The status is one of: scheduled, in progress, completed, missed or unscheduled. If a manager fixed your times, the day shows **Corrected** with the reason.

If you clock in with no shift planned, the app says your manager will review that clock-in.

## Common problems
- **I forgot to clock out.** Tell your manager. They can correct the record.
- **The button says "Checking…".** Wait a moment. Tap **Try again** if you see it.
- **I tapped twice.** The app tells you "You were already clocked in since ..." and does not double-count.
- **"Your login isn't linked to an employee record".** Ask your manager to fix your account.
- **No Clock menu item (Owner).** Owners have no employee record, so no personal clock.
