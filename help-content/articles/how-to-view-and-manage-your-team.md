**People** lists everyone you manage. A Branch Manager sees their own branch. A Company Admin or Owner sees the whole company. Open a person to see their details, documents, schedule, attendance and leave.

## Steps
1. Tap **People** in the menu.
2. Type a name or email in **Search by name or email**.
3. Use **Filter by status** and **Filter by branch** if you need to.
   ![People list with filters](shot:people-list)
4. Tap a name to open the record.
5. Use the tabs: **Overview**, **Employment**, **Documents**, **Schedule**, **Attendance**, **Leave**. Admins also see **Payslips**. Everyone sees **Audit**.
   ![Employee record with tabs](shot:people-employee-tabs)
6. To fix details, tap **Edit details** and then confirm the changes.
7. On **Overview**, look for **Pending change requests**. These come from staff.
8. Tap **Approve** or **Reject** next to each change.

## What happens next
A change shows as `old value → new value`. After you decide, the person sees **Approved** or **Rejected** in their change request history.

The record shows **Profile completeness** and what is **Missing**. Use **Ask for missing details** to ask the employee to fill gaps.

Status labels on People are: Active, Pending activation, Inactive and more. A Branch Manager can only move a person from pending activation to active. Company Admins and Owners can also use **Activate**, **Deactivate**, **Transfer**, **Start onboarding** and **Start offboarding** on the record.

## Common problems
- **I cannot find someone.** Check **Filter by status** and **Filter by branch** are set to "All statuses" and "All branches". Branch Managers only see their own branch.
- **I cannot see pay or bank details.** Only admins can see those.
- **I do not see Edit details.** Staff cannot edit. Managers can edit only some fields, such as phone.
