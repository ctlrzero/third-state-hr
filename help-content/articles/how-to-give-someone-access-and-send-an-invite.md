A person needs a login before they can use the app. You give access in **Admin**, then send the invite email. They tap the link and set a password.

## Steps
1. Tap **Admin** in the menu, then the **Users & access** tab.
2. Tap **Grant access**.
   ![Users and access list](shot:admin-users-tab)
3. Type their **Email**.
4. Choose a **Role**: Owner, Company Admin, Branch Manager, Shift Supervisor or Employee. The hint under the box explains each one.
5. Choose the **Company** and **Branch** if asked.
6. Choose the **Linked employee**. Only employees who have no login are listed.
7. Tap **Grant access**.
   ![Grant access form](shot:admin-grant-access)
8. In the list, tap **Send invite** next to the person.
9. If they never used the link, tap **Resend invite**.

To turn access off:
1. Tap **Revoke access** next to the person.
2. Type the **Reason**.
3. Tap **Revoke access** to confirm.

## What happens next
The app says "Invitation email sent to ...". If the person already has a login, it says access is applied and they can sign in now.

The list shows **Invite not sent yet** or **Invite not yet accepted**, and **Last sign-in**. When the person taps the link they choose a password. See the sign-in guide.

After you revoke access the person sees "Your access is inactive" when they sign in.

Role summary: Employees use their own schedule, clock, leave, documents and payslips. A Shift Supervisor is an employee who also runs the floor at their branch and cannot see pay, leave, documents or profiles, or build the schedule. A Branch Manager runs one branch. A Company Admin runs one company. The Owner has full access. Only the Owner can grant Owner access.

## Common problems
- **"Grant access to ... first, then send the invite."** You must grant access before you invite.
- **"Too many emails have been sent recently."** Wait a few minutes, then use **Resend invite**.
- **The person cannot find the email.** Ask them to check spam, then resend.
- **The linked employee is missing.** They already have a login, or have no record yet. Add them in **People**.
- **A supervisor needs a branch.** Set the employee's home branch first.
