Use the **Leave** page to ask for days off. Your manager decides. You can see your balance and every request you made.

## Steps
1. Tap **Leave** in the menu. The page is called **My leave**.
2. Look at **Balances** to see how many days you have.
   ![My leave page with balances](shot:leave-my-leave)
3. Tap **Request leave**.
4. Choose a **Leave type**: Annual Leave or Sick Leave.
5. Choose the **Start date**.
6. Choose the **End date**.
7. Check the line "You have ... days of ... left."
8. Add a **Reason (optional)**.
9. Tap **Submit request**.
   ![Request leave form](shot:leave-request-form)

To cancel a request:
1. Find it under **Your requests**.
2. Tap **Cancel** (only shown while it is Pending).
3. Confirm in the box.

## What happens next
The app says "Leave request submitted — your manager needs to approve it." Your request shows **Pending**.

The branch manager is told about your request. They can **Approve** or **Reject** it. Owners and Company Admins can also decide.

- **Approved**: your balance goes down and you are told with a notification.
- **Rejected**: you are told, and a manager's note may show under the request.
- **Cancelled**: you cancelled it. Any balance held for it is given back.

If you ask for more days than you have, the form warns "This is more than your balance — your manager may refuse it." Only an Owner or Company Admin can approve with an override.

You cannot cancel a request once it is Approved or Rejected. Talk to your manager.

## Common problems
- **"Leave isn’t set up for you yet."** Ask your manager to set up your leave.
- **The end date error.** The end date must be the same as or after the start date.
- **There is no Cancel button.** Only Pending requests can be cancelled.
- **I need leave for a day I am sick.** You can also use **Can’t come in** on your shift. It sends a leave request for you.
