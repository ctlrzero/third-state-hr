You can change any shift. A shift that is not shared yet can be edited freely. A shared shift needs a reason, and every change is kept in the history.

## Steps
1. Open **Schedules** and tap the shift on the week board.
   ![Shift actions panel](shot:schedules-shift-actions)
2. Read the note. It says "Not shared yet" or "Shared with staff".
3. To change it, tap **Edit** (or **Edit (with a reason)** for a shared shift).
4. Change **Branch**, **Assigned to**, **Date**, **Start**, **End** or **Break (min)**.
5. For a shared shift, type the **Reason for change**.
6. Tap **Save changes**.
   ![Change shift form](shot:schedules-change-shift)
7. To cancel, tap **Cancel shift** (or **Cancel shift (with a reason)**).
8. Type the **Reason** and tap **Cancel shift**.
9. To see what changed, tap **Change history**.

## What happens next
A changed shared shift shows **Changed** on the board. A cancelled shift shows **Cancelled** and stays for the record. History words include Cancelled, Reassigned, Date changed, Time changed, Branch changed, Break changed and "Can’t come in (reported)".

Admins can remove a cancelled shift for good with **Delete cancelled shift**. **Setup** has an **Open log** button that lists every deleted shift, who deleted it and why.

If a person reported **Can’t come in**, their shift shows it, and a leave request is waiting for you to decide.

## Common problems
- **The Save button will not work.** A shared shift needs a **Reason for change**.
- **I cancelled by mistake.** A cancelled shift cannot be undone. Create a new shift.
- **I do not see Delete cancelled shift.** Only Company Admins and Owners can delete.
