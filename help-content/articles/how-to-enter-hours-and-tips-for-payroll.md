Branch Managers do not see pay amounts. You enter and confirm the hours and overtime for your branch. The payroll admin does the rest.

## Steps
1. Tap **Payroll inputs** in the menu. The page is called **Payroll inputs**.
2. Choose the **Month**.
   ![Payroll inputs sheet](shot:payroll-hours-sheet)
3. Tap **Load from attendance**.
4. Check each person's hours and overtime.
5. Change a number if needed.
6. Tap **Save and confirm** for one person, or **Save** to keep it as a draft.
7. Tap **Confirm all pending** when everything is right.

## What happens next
Confirmed hours are used when payroll is prepared. The payroll admin sees them under **Hours this month** with **Hours confirmed** ticked.

If you see "No payroll month open", the payroll admin must open the month first.

## Common problems
- **The numbers look wrong.** Correct clock records first in **Attendance**, then load again.
- **I cannot see salaries.** That is on purpose. Only payroll admins see pay.
