Managers and admins start on **Home**. The **Action centre** is your inbox. It lists leave requests, document renewals, profile change requests and shift swaps that wait for you.

## Steps
1. Tap **Home** in the menu.
2. Look at the cards: **Employees**, **Documents expiring in 30 days**, **Pending leave requests**, and **Current payroll** for admins.
   ![Home dashboard](shot:manager-home)
3. Read the **Action centre** list. Each row shows the type, for example **Leave request**, **Document renewal**, **Profile change request** or **Shift swap**.
4. Tap **Open** on a row.
5. Decide on the page that opens.
6. Check **Operational coverage** for your branches, and tap **Open schedule →** to plan shifts.

## What happens next
After you decide, the item leaves the list. Opening an item takes you to the right page: **Leave** for leave, **Documents** for renewals, the person's record for profile changes, and **Schedules** for swaps.

The list shows the first 8 items. If it is empty you see "Nothing needs attention right now".

At a branch, use **Today** for live issues like late people. It also lists approvals waiting for you.

## Common problems
- **I do not see the item I expect.** Branch Managers only see their own branch.
- **The count looks high.** Open each row. Items stay until someone decides.
- **I am a Shift Supervisor and there is no Home dashboard.** Supervisors see the staff Home and use **Today** instead.
