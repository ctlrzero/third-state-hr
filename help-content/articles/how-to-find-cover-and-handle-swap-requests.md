When someone cannot work, the app can show who is free and allowed to cover. You can pick a person yourself or send an offer to several people.

## Steps
To find cover:
1. Tap **Find cover** on the **Today** board, or tap the shift on **Schedules** and choose **Reassign / find cover**. For an empty shift choose **Find someone for this shift**.
   ![Find cover panel](shot:cover-find-sheet)
2. To pick yourself, stay on **Pick someone**. Only people who are free and allowed to work are listed.
3. Tap a person.
4. Type a **Note for the shift history** if asked.
5. Tap **Assign**.

To offer the shift to a few people:
1. Tap **Ask a few people**.
2. Tick the people to ask.
3. Check the **Message**. Edit it if you want.
4. Tap **Send offer to** and the number.
   ![Offer shift to a few people](shot:cover-offer-panel)

To decide a swap:
1. On **Schedules**, find **Swap requests awaiting your decision**.
2. Read who asks and who will cover.
3. Tap **Approve** or **Reject**.
   ![Swap requests awaiting your decision](shot:cover-swap-requests)

## What happens next
After **Assign** the app says "... now covers ...". The shift moves to that person.

An offer goes to the people you ticked. The first to accept gets the shift and you are notified. Others who were offered see it closed. Suggestions come from scheduling rules, and nothing is sent until you press Send.

Swaps: **Approve** says "Swap approved — the shift has been reassigned." **Reject** says "Swap rejected." Staff see Approved or Not approved. **Swap history** lists older swaps.

People who cannot work the shift are listed under **Not eligible** with the reason.

## Common problems
- **Nobody is eligible.** The app says so and shows reasons. Change the shift time or ask someone from another branch.
- **Staff cannot ask for cover.** Shift handovers are off. Admins can switch on "Let staff hand shifts to each other" in **Setup**.
- **The offer was not sent to someone.** The app lists who was skipped and why.
