Reports show your payroll in tables you can download. Older payroll runs from before the current payroll screen are kept as read-only history.

## Steps
To use reports:
1. Open **Payroll** and choose the month.
2. Tap **Reports**.
   ![Payroll reports panel](shot:payroll-reports-drawer)
3. Choose a report: **Monthly payroll register**, **Branch payroll cost**, **Payments and outstanding**, or **Tips, advances and deductions**.
4. Read the table.
5. Download it as a CSV file.

To see earlier runs:
1. Scroll to the bottom of **Payroll**.
2. Open the section **Earlier payroll runs**.
3. Open a run and tap a payslip to see or download it.
   ![Earlier payroll runs](shot:payroll-earlier-runs)

## What happens next
Reports are for the month you chose. Earlier runs are read-only: nothing can be edited. Their payslips stay downloadable. The section only shows if there are earlier runs.

## Common problems
- **No Reports button.** Reports need payroll report permission. Company Admins, Owners and accountants have it.
- **I cannot find Earlier payroll runs.** The company has no payroll from before the current screen.
- **I want to change an old run.** You cannot. Make a correction in a current month if needed.
