If a payroll record is wrong, how you fix it depends on how far it went. Before payment and publishing you can return it to draft. After that you create a correction.

## Steps
To return a record to draft:
1. Open **Payroll** and tap the person.
2. Tap **Return to draft**.
3. Type a **Reason**.
4. Tap **Confirm**.
   ![Return to draft reason box](shot:payroll-return-draft)
5. Change what is wrong, for example in **Hours this month** or **One-off earnings and deductions**.
6. Tap **Recalculate**, then approve again.

To create a correction:
1. Open the person's record. It is **Approved** and already paid or published.
2. Tap **Create correction**.
3. Type the **Reason** and tap **Confirm**.
4. Fix the amounts on the new correction record.
5. Approve it, record any extra payment, and publish it.

To remove a one-off earning or deduction you added: tap **Remove** next to it.

## What happens next
**Return to draft** gives the reason on the record ("Returned to draft: ..."). Any exported payment list containing the person becomes void.

A correction is a new record marked **Correction**, with the reason. Payments already made count toward it, so only the difference is paid. The old payslip is marked as replaced ("This payslip was replaced by a later correction"), and the employee's new payslip is marked **Corrected**. Versions are listed under **Versions**.

A frozen record cannot be edited. The page says "This record is frozen. Return it to draft, or — once published or paid — create a correction instead."

## Common problems
- **Return to draft is greyed.** A payment is recorded or the payslip is published. The hint says "Create a correction instead."
- **Create correction is missing.** You may lack payroll prepare permission.
- **Someone was underpaid.** Use **Add earning or deduction** with type **Correction (underpaid)**, or create a correction.
