Your payslip shows what you earned and what was taken off. It appears after payroll is approved and your payslip is published.

## Steps
1. Tap **Payslips** in the menu. The page is called **My payslips**.
2. Tap the month you want.
   ![My payslips list](shot:payslips-list)
3. Read **Earnings**, **Allowances** and **Deductions**.
4. Check **Gross pay**, **Total deductions** and **Net pay** at the bottom.
   ![Payslip detail](shot:payslips-detail)
5. Tap **Download** to save a PDF.
6. Or tap **Open to print** to open the PDF and print it.

## What happens next
Each payslip shows your **Net pay**. A payslip can show a small label:
- **Corrected**: payroll fixed a mistake.
- **Updated**: the payslip was revised.

If a payslip was replaced, you see "This payslip was replaced by a later correction." Open the newer one instead.

If your pay is going to a new account, send your details first. See the profile guide.

## Common problems
- **"No payslips yet".** Your payslip only appears once payroll for the period is approved and published. Ask HR for the pay date.
- **My payslip looks wrong.** Tell your manager or HR. They can correct it. You will get a new payslip marked **Corrected**.
- **The PDF does not open.** Try **Open to print**, or use another browser.
