When you are hired you see **Onboarding** in the menu. It lists the steps to finish before and after you start. You can save and come back later.

## Steps
1. Tap **Onboarding** in the menu, or tap **Open** on the Home card "Complete your joining steps".
   ![Your joining steps](shot:onboarding-my-steps)
2. Step 1, **Your details**: fill in the boxes and tap **Save for later**, or tap **Send to HR** when all required boxes are done.
3. Step 2, **Documents**: choose a file for each document and tap **Upload**.
4. Step 3, **Salary payment**: choose **How you are paid**, then fill **Account holder name** and **IBAN**.
5. Step 4, **Company policies**: open each policy and accept it.
6. Step 5, **Employment contract**: tap to view it, tick the agreement box and tap **Accept contract**.
7. Look for **When you can work**. Tick the days you can work, then tap **Confirm availability**.
   ![When you can work](shot:onboarding-availability)

## What happens next
Each step shows **To do**, **Done** or **Accepted**. The progress bar shows **Your joining steps**.

Your manager reviews what you send. Documents and pay details can come back as **Changes required**. Fix them and send again. When everything is ready, your manager activates your account. After that Home shows **Your first weeks** and **Your onboarding checklist**.

Your availability helps your manager plan your shifts.

## Common problems
- **"Send to HR" is greyed out.** A required box is empty. Hover or tap it to see "Still needed".
- **The IBAN is not accepted.** It starts with AE and has 23 characters.
- **I do not see Onboarding.** It only shows while you have an open onboarding.
- **The contract says "Being prepared".** HR has not uploaded it yet.
