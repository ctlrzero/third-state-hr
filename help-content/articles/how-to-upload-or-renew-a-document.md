Your manager may ask for a document such as a passport copy or visa. You may also need to send a new copy when a document is about to expire. You do both in **Documents**.

## Steps
To upload a document your manager asked for:
1. Open **Home**.
2. Under **Your manager needs something**, tap **Upload document**.
   ![Home card asking for a document](shot:docs-home-request)
3. The upload form opens. Check the **Document type**.
4. Choose the **File**. Only PDF, JPG and PNG files work.
5. Add an **Expiry date (optional)** if the document has one.
6. Add **Notes (optional)** if you need to.
7. Tap **Upload**.
   ![Upload document form](shot:docs-upload-form)

You can also open **Documents** in the menu. Under **Your manager needs these**, tap **Upload**.

To renew an expiring document:
1. Open **Documents**. The page is called **My documents**.
2. Find the document under **Your documents**.
3. Tap **Upload new copy**.
   ![My documents with Upload new copy](shot:docs-my-documents)
4. Add the **New expiry date (optional)**.
5. Choose the **File**, then submit.

To fix a rejected document:
1. Find it under **Your manager needs these**. It says **Rejected** and shows the reason.
2. Tap **Resubmit**.

## What happens next
After you upload, the app says "Uploaded — pending review." Your document shows **Pending review**. Someone else must check it. You cannot approve your own upload. A manager or admin then approves or rejects it.

- **Approved**: your document is saved as current.
- **Rejected**: the reason is shown. Send a new file with **Resubmit**.
- **Expiring** or **Expired**: you can upload a new copy. You can only upload a new copy when it is expiring or expired.

When you renew, your old approved copy stays current until the new one is approved. You see "Your renewal is pending review."

Tap **View** to open a file.

## Common problems
- **"Only PDF, JPG and PNG files are accepted."** Convert or take a photo and save it as JPG or PNG.
- **I do not see Upload new copy.** The document is not close to expiry yet. It shows 60 days before it expires.
- **My renewal was rejected.** Read the reason, then use **Resubmit**.
- **"The upload did not complete — please try again."** Check your internet and try again.
- **Some document types are not visible to me.** Passport, visa, contract and similar documents are only handled by Company Admins and Owners.
