**Attendance** compares planned shifts with real clock-ins. You can fix mistakes. Every correction keeps the original record and the reason.

## Steps
1. Tap **Attendance** in the menu.
2. Choose **Branch**, **From** and **To**, then tap **Refresh**.
3. Open the **Exceptions** tab. Supervisors see this view only.
   ![Attendance exceptions](shot:attendance-exceptions)
4. Find the problem. Types are: **Missing clock-out**, **Didn’t clock in**, **Clock-in without a shift**, **Late clock-in**.
5. If you see **Suggested clock-out**, tap **Confirm** to accept the time. Tap **Dismiss** to ignore it. Tap **Change…** to set another time.
6. Otherwise tap **Correct**.
7. Set **New clock-in (Dubai time)** and **New clock-out (Dubai time)**. Leave a box empty to keep it.
8. Type the **Reason**. The employee can see it.
9. Tap **Review changes**, then **Save correction**.
   ![Correct attendance form](shot:attendance-correct-form)

To change paid hours (managers and admins):
1. Open the **Overview** tab.
2. Tap **Change paid hours** on a row.
3. Set the new **Hours** and **Minutes**, and a **Reason**.
4. Tap **Send for approval**.
5. Open **Hours changes** to see the request. Approvers tap **Apply** or **Reject**.

## What happens next
After a correction the app says the original record is kept in the audit trail. The employee sees **Corrected** and your reason in their last 30 days.

Hours changes show **Pending**, then applied or rejected. Rejecting needs a **Reason for rejecting**. If a shift is already in payroll, **Change paid hours** is off ("Already in payroll").

The tiles show **Shifts in range**, **Exceptions**, **Hours changes waiting** and **Hours to pay**. If there are no payable shifts, tap **Load hours from shifts**.

## Common problems
- **"You cannot correct your own record."** Ask another manager.
- **"Times cannot be in the future."** Pick a time in the past.
- **"No clock-in to correct".** The person never clocked in. Ask them to clock in, or tell payroll.
- **I do not see Overview or Hours changes.** Shift supervisors only see exceptions and corrections, never pay time.
