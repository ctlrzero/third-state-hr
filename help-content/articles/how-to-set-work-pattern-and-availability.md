A work pattern tells the schedule how many days a person works and when they have days off. Shift forms and Auto-schedule use it to warn you or plan correctly.

## Steps
1. Open **People** and tap the person.
2. Open the **Schedule** tab.
3. Find the **Work pattern** card.
   ![Work pattern card](shot:work-pattern-card)
4. Tap **Set pattern** (or **Edit**). Company Admins and Owners can edit it.
5. Choose **Working days per week**.
6. Under **Days off**, choose **Flexible** (the scheduler decides) or **Fixed days** (always the same days). For fixed days, tap the days.
7. Choose **Shift type**: **Any shift** or **Morning only** (shifts that start before 10:00).
8. Tap **Save pattern**.
   ![Work pattern editor](shot:work-pattern-editor)

## What happens next
The card says "Saved. The auto-scheduler and shift forms now use this pattern."

With no pattern, the scheduler allows up to 6 days a week, on any day. A broken pattern does not block you: the shift form shows "You can still save — this is a heads-up, not a block."

Below the pattern, **Availability** shows each day: **Day off**, **Unavailable**, a time range, **Any time** or **Not set**. Staff give this themselves in their onboarding step "When you can work". If none is on file, the person counts as available any day and any time.

To remove a pattern, use the remove option on the card and confirm.

## Common problems
- **I cannot see Set pattern.** Only Company Admins and Owners can edit it.
- **I cannot change someone's availability.** The page only shows it. We could not find a screen where staff change it after onboarding.
- **Auto-schedule ignores a day.** Check that person's days off and availability.
