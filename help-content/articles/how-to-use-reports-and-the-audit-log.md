**Reports** shows quick counts and the **Audit log**. The audit log lists changes made in the app, who made them and when.

## Steps
1. Tap **Reports** in the menu. The page is called **Reports & audit**.
2. Read the cards at the top: **Active employees**, **Open requisitions**, **Documents pending review**, **Leave requests pending**, **Open shifts**, **Swaps awaiting approval**. Admins also see **Payroll runs in progress**.
   ![Reports metrics](shot:reports-metrics)
3. Scroll to **Audit log**.
4. Use **Module**, **Record type**, **Action**, **From** and **To** to narrow the list. Admins can also pick **Actor**, **Employee**, **Branch** and **Entity**.
   ![Audit log filters](shot:reports-audit-log)
5. Tap **Export CSV** to download the list. Only Company Admins and Owners see this button.

## What happens next
Each row shows what changed and who did it. Branch Managers only see their own branch. Settings changes are visible to the Owner only.

## Common problems
- **I do not see Export CSV.** Only Company Admins and Owners can export.
- **Nothing shows.** Clear the filters, or widen the dates.
- **I cannot see another branch.** Branch Managers are limited to their branch.
