When a person is missing documents, bank details or personal details, you can ask them in one go. The app only asks for the items you tick. The request appears on the employee's Home screen.

## Steps
To ask one employee:
1. Open **People** and tap the person's name.
2. Find the card **Requests to** their name.
3. Tap **Ask for missing details**.
   ![Requests card on an employee record](shot:ask-requests-card)
4. Tick what to ask for under **Documents**, **Bank details** and **Personal details**. Tap **Select all** to tick everything.
5. Add a **Note for them (optional)**.
6. Add a **Due date (optional)**.
7. Tap **Ask for** and the number of items.
   ![Ask for missing details tick-list](shot:ask-missing-ticklist)

To ask for one thing only:
1. On the same card, tap **Ask for something**.
2. Choose **Upload a document**, **Bank / payment details** or **Missing information**.
3. Choose the document or tick the details.
4. Tap **Send request**.

To ask everyone:
1. Open **People**.
2. Set **Filter by branch** if you want only one branch.
3. Tap **Ask for missing details** at the top.
4. Tick the items, then tap the **Ask for** button.
   ![Ask everyone tick-list](shot:ask-many-ticklist)

## What happens next
The app says who was asked. The employee sees **Your manager needs something** on Home with a button such as **Upload document**, **Add bank details** or **Fill in details**.

On the employee record, open requests show **Waiting**, or **Overdue** after the due date. Tap **Cancel** to withdraw one. Tap **Show finished requests** to see **Done** and **Cancelled** requests.

Each person is only asked for what they are actually missing. Items already asked show "Already asked" and cannot be ticked again. If nothing is missing, the app says the person "isn’t missing anything".

Branch Managers cannot ask for sensitive documents. These show "Owner or Company Admin only". When a Branch Manager asks for everyone, only their own branch is included.

## Common problems
- **The tick box is greyed out.** It says "Already asked", or only an Owner or Company Admin can ask for it.
- **"Nothing new to send".** Nobody is missing the ticked items, or they have already been asked.
- **The employee says they cannot see it.** Ask them to open **Home**. The request is there.
- **I cannot choose a passport.** Passport, visa, Emirates ID and contracts can only be asked for by a Company Admin or Owner.
