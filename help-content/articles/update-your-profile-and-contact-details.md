Your profile shows your job, branch and contact details. Some details need approval before they change. If your manager asks for missing details or bank details, you fill them in here.

## Steps
1. Tap **Profile** in the menu. The page is called **My profile**.
2. Find **Contact details**.
   ![My profile page](shot:my-profile-contact)
3. Next to the detail you want to change, tap **Request change**. You can change Phone number, Email address, Emergency contact name and Emergency contact phone.
4. Type the **New value**.
5. Add a **Reason (optional)**.
6. Tap **Submit request**.
   ![Request a change form](shot:my-profile-request-change)

To fill in details your manager asked for:

1. On **Home**, tap **Fill in details** (or **Add bank details**) under **Your manager needs something**.
2. You land on **Please fill in your missing details**.
3. Fill in the boxes, for example **Your phone number**, **Date of birth**, **Nationality**, **Home address in the UAE**.
4. Tap **Save my details**.
   ![Missing details form](shot:my-profile-missing-details)

To send bank details:

1. Open **Your bank / payment details**.
2. Choose **How do you want to be paid?**: Bank account (IBAN), Salary card (WPS), Exchange house or Cash.
3. Fill in the bank boxes. For a bank account the **IBAN** is needed.
4. Tap **Send bank details**.
   ![Bank details form](shot:my-profile-bank-details)

## What happens next
A change request shows as **Pending** in **Change request history**. An owner, admin or manager must approve it. It then shows as Approved or Rejected, and may show a note from the person who decided.

Missing details are saved straight away. If more are still missing the app says so. Bank details go to payroll, who check them before pay day. New bank details replace older ones.

Your job, employment type and dates are managed by HR. You cannot change them here. **Document expiry** shows your Labor card and Health card dates. To renew them, go to **Documents**.

## Common problems
- **There is no Request change button.** Only the four contact fields can be changed. Ask HR for anything else.
- **My request is still Pending.** Wait for a manager or admin to review it.
- **I see "No employee record linked to your account yet".** Ask an owner or admin to link your login to your employee record.
- **The IBAN is not accepted.** It starts with AE and has 23 characters. Spaces are fine.
